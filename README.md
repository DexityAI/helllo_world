# Dexity

> Skills you can deploy Monday morning.

Dexity is AI upskilling focused on your career and outcomes — not certificates and not theory.

Live 4–6 week sprints, taught by practitioners actively in the role today. You pick one role-specific skill, build a real project, and leave with a clear next move.

---

## What we're building

A focused AI skills platform for working professionals — across product, engineering, marketing, design, sales, finance, HR, ops, legal, and healthcare.

Every sprint is one deployable skill, taught by someone doing that job today, ending with a production-grade project you can use in your work, your promotion case, or your next interview.

No theory dumps. No 6-month curricula. No certificate factories.

## How it works

1. **Learn** — live cohort sessions with a practitioner currently in the role at a top-tier company
2. **Build** — a production-grade project you can show in a promotion or hiring conversation
3. **Deploy** — take it into your work immediately

Sprints run 4–6 weeks. Live and cohort-based. Designed around your current role.
